# Original provider identifiers (MIT)

These four SVGs were drawn from scratch for this project during the sharing review on 2026-11-08 (ICT). They are simple neutral geometric letters, not copies, tracings, or imitations of official logos. They use a common square grid and the chart text color, with no official artwork or font outlines.

The identifiers are G for Grok, C for Claude (also the Fable display alias), C with a square inset for Codex, and A for AGY. Historical filenames `openai.svg` and `antigravity.svg` remain renderer keys only; they do not imply official artwork. The original identifier artwork is distributed under the project's MIT license in `LICENSE`. No third-party logo artwork remains in this directory.

Provider names identify compatible services. Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners; no sponsorship, endorsement, or trademark license is implied.

All artwork bounds are `(0, 0, 10, 10)`. The renderer verifies SHA-256 before parsing and caches verified templates. Missing/modified assets fail explicitly. Only passive allowlisted SVG elements and attributes with document-local references are accepted; scripts, HTML/foreignObject, styles, processing instructions, entities, href/image resources and external URLs are rejected. No asset fetch or font download occurs. Both the resvg path and lazy Matplotlib fallback use these original paths. Marks remain seven logical pixels high beside provider headers.
